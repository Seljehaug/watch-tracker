Watch Tracker – screens at phone, tablet and desktop

Examples and inspiration, not an answer key (DESIGN.md, D26). The rules are the tokens (§1), the accessibility requirements and the behaviour notes; the measurements here are one way to follow them. Each frame is a fixed-size picture of one width – phone 390 × 844, tablet 768 × 1024, desktop 1280 × 800 – not one responsive page. Values named --mock-* are not tokens yet.

Home – Continue watching

Phone: one column, scrolling tab row, floating add button. Tablet and desktop: the card grid from DESIGN.md §3 (2 columns at 768px, 4 at 1280px), title and tabs on one row when they fit, and "Add show" in the header instead of the floating button.

Add/edit show

Phone: the action bar stays at the bottom of the screen. Tablet and desktop: one narrower column, centred (D39), and the buttons follow the last field instead of sitting at the bottom of the window. No "Back" link on any size: the header shows only the brand, and Cancel next to Save is the way out (D52). The steppers are as wide as their content, so − and + stay next to the number.

Delete dialog

Phone: a bottom sheet. Tablet and desktop: centred over the form. The buttons stay stacked in the same order on every size, so the focus order never differs from what you see. Focus goes to Cancel when it opens (DESIGN.md §2).

Empty state

No floating add button on phone and no "Add show" in the header on wider screens: the empty state's own button is the one way forward.

Not found

One page for a URL no route matches and for an edit URL whose show isn't in the list; the address bar keeps the URL, so the text covers both (D53). The empty state's look in the page's own markup: the heading is the page's <h1>, at the page title size (D55). The page-not-found icon (the TV with a cross), not the alert icon – there is nothing to correct. The header is the same as on Home; no floating add button on phone, as the page's own button is the way forward.
