Watch Tracker – screens and states

Examples and inspiration, not an answer key (DESIGN.md, D26). The rules are the tokens (§1), the accessibility requirements and the behaviour notes; the measurements here are one way to follow them. Each frame is a fixed-size picture of one width and one state – phone 390 × 844, tablet 768 × 1024, desktop 1280 × 800 – not one responsive page. Frames marked "the whole page" show everything a scroll would reveal. Values named --mock-* are not tokens yet.

The look is provisional (D103). What these screens decide is the structure, the states and the behaviour: navigation (D98), Watching and Library (D99), search (D100), the show page (D101–D102) and the show card's stepper (D114). Colours, type, spacing and the treatment of images get a proper design pass later, and much of it may change. Show titles, years and streaming services are sample data, and the grey rectangles stand in for TMDB's posters and backdrops.

Lists

Watching (Home, /)

Only the shows being watched, most recently updated first, in the order they had when the page opened (D36). The stepper at the bottom of each card – − · progress · + in one quiet pill – marks the next episode or goes back one (D114); the poster slot is as tall as the text, and its images come in M3 (D115). The status tabs are on Library now (D99). Phone: the header holds the brand and the account icon, and the bottom navigation – Watching · Library · Search, router links with aria-current="page" – sits at the bottom (D98). From 48rem (tablet and desktop) there is no bottom navigation: the header has the links, the search field on the right and the account icon. The whole card opens the show page (D101), except the stepper's buttons.

Watching – nothing on the go

The empty state's button goes to Search, where shows are added (D100). The navigation stays, so Library and Search are still one tap away.

Watching in M4 – ready to watch and caught up

A sketch, decided in M4 (D99). With TMDB's episode data the list splits in two: shows with aired episodes after yours, and shows where you have seen everything that is out. No separate "Up next" page. Caught-up shows show their progress without the stepper, as the next episode isn't out. Whether the card shows the last or the next episode is part of M4 (D35).

Library (/library/want-to-watch, /finished, /dropped)

The rest of your shows, one status at a time: the status tabs from DESIGN.md §2 as router links, one route per status, opening on Want to watch (D99). Watching isn't a tab here – it has its own place. The + on a Want to watch card starts the show at S01 E01 (D34), and its − is disabled; Finished and Dropped cards show their progress as plain text where the stepper would be (D114). A tab with no shows says "Nothing here yet."

Search

Search – first visit

The first time the page opens, a tip explains the two icon buttons and the manual way. "Got it" hides it for good – remembered in localStorage behind a small service, per device (D100) – and moves focus to the search field. Phone: the search field takes the header's place, has focus when the page opens and the keyboard is up (not the <h1>, which is visually hidden here). Desktop: the header's field has focus and is wide.

Search – before typing

After the tip is gone. The manual way is here from the start, as a button.

Search – loading

Results come as you type, after a short pause (debounced), with replaceUrl so Back leaves the search in one step. Shows already in your list are matched locally and can show at once; TMDB's results replace the placeholder rows. "Searching…" is in the same polite live region as the count.

Search – results (/search?q=the+office)

Each result: poster, title (wrapping to two lines, never cut), years, country and channel – four shows are called The Office – and seasons; the availability line arrives in M4. A show in your list says so ("In your list · Watching · S04 E12") and has no buttons. Others have two icon buttons (D100): the bookmark adds to Want to watch, the plus to Watching without progress. Their accessible names carry the title, country and year: "Add The Office (UK, 2001) to Watching". The whole result opens the show page; the buttons sit above that link (as on the card, D51). "Add manually" is on the count line and again under the results, with the query as the title.

Search – after adding

The plus on the British version, once the server has answered. The result now says "In your list · Watching · Not started", and a message (role="status") says what happened, with "Set progress" to the show page. It stays about 10 seconds, and as long as it is hovered or has focus. It isn't the only way on – the result links to the show page too (WCAG 2.2.1). From the bookmark the message says "Added The Office to Want to watch".

Search – no results

The manual way becomes the main button, with the query as the title (/shows/add?title=…).

Search – TMDB or the connection fails

Says what went wrong and what to do; "Try again" repeats the search. Shows in your list still match, as they are searched locally.

Search – the field opening

Desktop: at rest the field is wide enough to read "Search shows", with / as a shortcut; with focus it grows to the left into the empty space, and typing shows the results page below. Phone: tapping Search in the bottom navigation opens the search page, and the icon grows into the field at the top – the icon and the field share a view-transition-name, and the router's View Transitions (withViewTransitions()) animate between them. No movement under prefers-reduced-motion, for either.

Show page

Show page – in your list (/shows/:showId)

Two parts (D101): what the show is, and "Your show" – everything you can change, saved field by field as you change it (D102). Phone order: image and title, Your show, Where to watch (M4), About, Seasons – Your show comes early, as you usually come from the list to update. Desktop: the backdrop across the top, the poster on the left, the info in the middle, and Your show with Where to watch in a column on the right that stays in view (position: sticky). The title sits below the image on phones, so it never lands on a light picture. Both images are decorative (alt=""): the title is next to them. No cast, trailers, reviews or "similar shows" (§1).

Show page – saving, an invalid episode and a failed save

Scrolled down to Your show. Selects save as soon as you pick; the steppers about 600 ms after the last tap; the title of a manual show when you leave the field. One status line for the whole part, in a polite live region: "Saving…", then "Saved". With only one of season and episode filled, nothing is saved and the error from D44 shows right away – there is no save attempt to wait for here. A failed save keeps what you chose and offers "Try again".

Show page – not in your list (/shows/tmdb/:tmdbId)

Opened from a search result. The address uses TMDB's id, as the show has no id of our own until it is added. The two buttons stand where Your show would be, with text, as there is room. After adding, the address becomes the show's own (/shows/:showId, replaceUrl) and Your show appears in their place. A TMDB address for a show already in your list sends on to its own address the same way.

Show page – added manually

No TMDB data: no images, About, Seasons or Where to watch. Your show gets the title field, and "Find on TMDB" links the show to a TMDB id (M3) – after that it looks like any other show.

Delete dialog

Opened from "Delete show" in Your show (D59). No icon tile (D84). Phone: a bottom sheet that slides up (D84, built last). Wider: centred. Cancel first, then Delete show – the same order in the DOM; in a row, centred, and stacked with Cancel on top when the dialog is narrower than 22rem. After deleting, the app goes to the list the show was in.

Other

Add a show manually (/shows/add)

Reached from Search only (D100); the title comes from the query. The form keeps an explicit button, as it creates something. Errors show after the first try to add (D41). Cancel goes back to the search. No bottom navigation on phones: the form's action bar has the bottom. Tablet and desktop: one narrower column, centred (D39).

Account (/account)

Reached from the account icon in the header (D98); the icon gets aria-current="page" here, and nothing in the bottom navigation is current. Log out is a button – it is an action. "About" credits TMDB with its logo and the sentence TMDB asks for, and JustWatch for the where-to-watch data. Later: My subscriptions and export.

Log in (/login)

M2 C2. Only the brand in the header, no navigation. One narrow, centred column on every size. A wrong email or password shows one message below the button (role="alert"), never which of the two was wrong (D129).

Not found

One page for a URL no route matches and for a show id that isn't in your list – the guard moves to /shows/:showId (D53, D101). The navigation stays, with nothing current.
